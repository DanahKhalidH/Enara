# Enara
Enara

Project Overview

Enara is a smart digital tourism platform designed to help users discover and book marine and land activities across Saudi Arabia. The platform connects tourists and adventure seekers with local activity providers, making it easier to explore destinations and plan trips.

Problem Statement

Tourists may find it difficult to discover suitable activities, compare experiences, and organize their trips in one place. Enara aims to simplify this process by bringing activity discovery, trip planning, and booking together in a single platform.

Main Features

• Explore Activities: Discover marine and land experiences, including diving, beach activities, camping, hiking, and exploration tours.
• Trip Planning: Plan trips and choose activities based on personal interests.
• Activity Booking: Book available activities directly through the platform.
• AI-Based Recommendations: Provide personalized activity and route suggestions based on user interests and weather conditions.
• Service Provider Dashboard: Allow local providers to manage activities, bookings, and offers.
